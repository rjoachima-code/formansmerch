Men's Apparel Merchandising & Sizing SOP

Men's Department Training

SOP-611-MENS
Floor Presentation Expectations

The Men's Department is a high-velocity pad. Associates assigned to Men's are pad owners and must keep it presentation-ready.

Critical Sizing Laws:
  • Denim Bottoms: Sorted left-to-right (Smallest waist 28 up to 44). Rigid plastic disk dividers must separate waist sizes.
  • Clearance Zone: Must be completely size-sequenced. Purge empty hangers routinely to prevent clutter.
  • Hosiery: Peg blocks must have package labels faced perfectly. Pick up trash and loose items daily.
Coaching Points & Warnings

Spend first 2 hours running racks. Maintain layout strictly based on Merchandise Manager decisions.

Big Men's department runs must have oversized visual tags to avoid mixing.

Women's Merchandising, Brand & Sleepwear SOP

Women's Department Training

SOP-611-WOMENS
Hanger Presentation & Brand Segregation

The Women's pad relies on style coordination, brand separation, and clean brand blocks. Hangers must look professional.

Critical Presentation Guidelines:
  • The Question-Mark Rule: Verify 100% of hanger hooks pointing to the left (forming a "?" shape).
  • Sleepwear & Lingerie: Ensure 2-piece coordinates are locked together on a single hanger to prevent mismatched sets.
  • Scrubs Pad: Must be grouped strictly by solid uniform color stories. Sizes must flow smoothly.
Coaching Points & Warnings

Do not allow JR brand lines to overlap Missy sizes. Maintain clear section barriers.

Check under bottom display base decks hourly. Sweep up fallen tickets, tags, and hangers.

Kids, Youth Growth & Footwear Layout SOP

Kids Department Training

SOP-611-KIDS
Growth Bracket Boundaries & Shoe Facings

The Kids department must remain strictly separated by age/size classifications. Muddled size runs can create visual friction for families.

Critical Kids Rules:
  • Growth Segment Boundaries: Maintain strict separation between Boys 8-20/4-7 and Girls 7-16/2T-6 pad segments.
  • Toddlers & Infants: Ensure matching toddler coordinate sets are securely matched/clipped. Sized run dividers must be updated.
  • Shoe Grid Box Facings: Stack box inventories symmetrically with product sizing labels facing outward.
Coaching Points & Warnings

Instruct associates to run racks to their assigned location without altering general department layouts.

Kids clearance pad must be sorted left-to-right strictly by size brackets.

Home Goods, Toys & Bedding Layout SOP

Home Goods Department Training

SOP-611-HOME
Shelf Edge facing & Stack safety

Hardlines present unique merchandising challenges. An un-faced shelf looks empty and unappealing. 100% execution of Z-lining is standard across all displays.

Critical Hardlines Rules:
  • The Z-Lining Standard: Always pull inventory forward so it sits flush to the absolute front edge of the shelves.
  • Box & Product Label Facing: Turn 100% of product packages, boxes, and towels with their front brand tags directly facing the customer.
  • Oversized/Heavy Toys Safety: Stack large boxes squarely on bottom decks. Never leave heavy items on top shelves where they could fall.
Coaching Points & Warnings

Organize towels, sheets, and pillows strictly by visual design stories and color continuity blocks.

Clear out stray toys, apparel, or empty boxes from home aisles routinely to maintain a safe walkthrough.

Front End, Showcase Security & Q-Line SOP

Front End Training

SOP-611-FRONT
Security controls & cashwrap standards

The front end is our final point of customer interaction and our primary line of defense against high-shrink items. Security controls and neat presentation are mandatory.

Critical Front End Rules:
  • Showcase Security: Keep luxury perfume and cologne tables fully secured. Glass cases must remain locked. The key group must stay physically attached to the manager's belt.
  • Q-Line Impulse Density: Impulse lane racks must be kept stocked, faced, and neatly aligned to encourage impulse buys. Enforce strict FIFO snacks rotation.
  • Return Baskets: Empty behind-the-counter return baskets hourly. Sort them into department bins so teams can return items to active rods.
Coaching Points & Warnings

Verify cashwrap counters are completely clear of trash, loose cardboard, and hangers. Keep registers pristine.

Do not allow handwritten value signs on cash wrap tables. Use only corporate promotional signage templates.

Backroom Logistics, Dock Doors & Plates Safety SOP

Logistics & Safety Department Training

SOP-611-LOGISTICS
Dock safety & warehouse securement laws

The backroom is a high-risk logistics center. Orderliness, proper egress pathways, and proper handling of mechanical dock plates are mandatory to prevent injury.

Critical Safety Rules:
  • The 36-Inch Rule: Maintain a strict 36-inch clearance around all emergency fire doors, electrical panels, and fire extinguishers. Keep exit routes completely clear.
  • Dock Plate Operation: Only certified trained personnel may operate the leveler or mechanical dock plates. Leverage bars must be stored in their correct safety brackets.
  • Dock Door Securement: Keep rolling dock doors completely closed and locked/latched when no active shipping trailer is backed in.
  • Pallet Stacking Limits: Wood pallets must be stacked flat, squared, and capped at a maximum safety ceiling of 12 pallets high.
Coaching Points & Warnings

Never process salvage items or freight in a way that blocks fire door exit routes or dock plate steps.

Incoming cargo pallets must be processed strictly using first-in, first-out (FIFO) logistics sequence based on arrival dates.
